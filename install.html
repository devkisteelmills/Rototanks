How to place and care for a tank

Base

  • Use a flat, level surface: wooden platform, smooth concrete plinth, or level river sand.
  • Do not lift a tank that still contains liquid.
  • Avoid dropping the tank, especially onto corners.

Sun and heat

  • Reduce long hours of direct sunlight where practical (shade, shade cloth).
  • Keep vegetation and fire hazards clear of the tank.
  • For chemical use, stay within the temperature limits advised for polyethylene.

Fittings

  • Use suitable flanges and connections for polyethylene tanks.
  • Store only liquids compatible with the tank grade.
